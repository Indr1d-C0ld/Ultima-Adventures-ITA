Whenever the map looks like that, it tends to be a Ultima Live issue. I don't know why some folks have had that problem and other's havent.

Generally the fix is:
> delete `%programdata%\Ultima Adventures`
> (AKA `C:\ProgramData\Ultima Adventures\`)


or for Linux, Per Luftwraith!

copied the map cache files over from the Wine C:\Program Data\Ultima Adventures\ from the TazUO ver packaged with the UA files into ~/.local/share/UltimaLive/
